# Evan: licence notice

Evan is an English voice for RHVoice, trained with Fasih's recipe on about 5.3 hours of speech (3,121 public-domain sentences from the LJ Speech transcripts) read by OpenEVV's "Adult Male 1" voice.

OpenEVV (github.com/Mudb0y/openevv) is IBM's Embedded ViaVoice text-to-speech engine, the one screen reader users know as Eloquence, rebuilt as portable C. Its engine code is its authors' own work under the MIT licence. But the language data that gives its voices their sound, the rules, tables and voice presets, was transcribed from IBM's Embedded ViaVoice objects and is IBM's work. OpenEVV's authors say they are in no position to license that data to anyone (see its NOTICE file).

Evan's models were learned from speech produced with that data. So the rights in Evan's voice are not Fasih's to grant: they are IBM's, or belong to whoever holds IBM's rights in Embedded ViaVoice today. The MIT and GPL licences elsewhere in Fasih do not cover this voice. It is offered as a separate, optional download, for personal and non-commercial use, and it will be withdrawn if the rights holder asks.
